## SECTOR 05 — Shipping for Real

**Title:** Freelance. Building for real humans.

**Intro copy:**
Since January 2026, I've been building AI agents, RAG systems, and chatbots for clients. The kind of systems where "it works in the notebook" gets you exactly nowhere. These need to handle edge cases, scale, and not hallucinate when a lawyer is reading the output.

**Case File 01: Legal Document RAG System**
*The problem:* Legal documents are dense, nested, and full of cross-references. Lawyers need precise answers with citations, not vibes.
*What I built:* A FastAPI backend with Qdrant for vector search, structure-aware chunking that respects articles, sections, and clauses — not just arbitrary 512-token blocks. Every answer comes back with the exact citation.
*The painful part:* Getting chunk boundaries right. Legal text doesn't care about your token limits.
